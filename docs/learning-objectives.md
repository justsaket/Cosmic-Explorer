# Educational Standards & Learning Objectives

## Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS) Alignment
- **MS-ESS1-1**: Develop and use a model of the Earth-Sun-Moon system to describe patterns of lunar phases, gravity, and orbital motion.
- **MS-ESS1-2**: Develop and use a model to describe the role of gravity in the motions within galaxies and the solar system.
- **MS-ESS1-3**: Analyze and interpret data to determine scale properties of objects in the solar system.
- **HS-ESS1-4**: Use mathematical or computational representations to predict the motion of orbiting objects in the solar system.
- **MS-ESS2-2**: Construct an explanation based on evidence for how geoscience processes have changed Earth's surface at varying time and spatial scales (e.g., impact cratering vs flowing water erosion on Mars).

## Core Concepts Taught
1. **Comparative Planetology**:
   - Contrast between the airless, micro-meteorite bombarded lunar surface vs the thin CO2 atmosphere and water-carved river deltas of ancient Mars.
2. **Gravitational Mechanics**:
   - Direct physical experience of 9.81 m/s² (Earth) vs 1.62 m/s² (Moon) vs 3.71 m/s² (Mars) and its effects on human mobility and leap height.
3. **Astrobiology**:
   - Criteria for past habitability: neutral pH standing water, clay mineral preservation of biosignatures, and organic carbon compounds.
